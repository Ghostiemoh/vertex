Welcome everyone. Today we are presenting Vertex, the surgical point of finality for Web3 freelance billing. We are building Stripe for the freelance crypto economy, fixing the massive operational fragmentation that occurs at the intersection of legal agreements and token settlements on Solana.
THE PROBLEM

Professional crypto billing is trapped in a fragmentation gap.

  • Intent Disconnect: Legal agreements (negotiated via Docs) are entirely isolated from actual on-chain transaction execution.
  • Verification Hell: Freelancers waste hours matching browser transaction hashes to spreadsheets to prove they got paid.
  • High Friction: Traditional fiat processors toll 3% in fees and impose 3-day delays on global contractor payments.
Speaker Notes
The problem is simple: professional crypto transactions are highly manual. When a project wraps up, the freelancer sends their address on Telegram, the client sends a payment, and then they cross-reference explorer hashes. There is no legal tie-in, no automation, and no invoice ledger. It's a compliance nightmare.
