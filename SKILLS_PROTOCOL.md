## 🧩 Multi-Skill Combination Rules

### 1. The "Analyst-First" Chain
- **Scenario**: Building a data-heavy feature.
- **Workflow**: `data-analysis` (Validate data) -> `prd-generator` (Define spec) -> `chart-designer` (Design UI) -> `clean-code` (Implement).

### 2. The "Prestige Content" Chain
- **Scenario**: Launching a marketing initiative.
- **Workflow**: `strategic-planning` (Define angle) -> `content-writer` (Draft copy) -> `antigravity-master-class` (Visuals) -> `twitter-automation` (Distribute).

### 3. The "Surgical Finality" Chain
- **Scenario**: Modifying invoicing/contract logic.
- **Workflow**: `contract-review` (Legal audit) -> `architecture-master-class` (System audit) -> `test-driven-development` (Safe build) -> `security-monitoring` (Verification).

## 🛡️ Guardrails
- **Minimalism**: Do not load redundant skills. Only summon what is necessary for the current step.
- **Autonomy**: Do not wait for the user to name a skill. Identify the "Task Type" and act immediately.
- **Context Primacy**: Always read `FOUNDER_CONTEXT.md` to ensure outputs are personalized to the **Serial Creator** identity.

## 🎨 The "Shadow Sketch" Protocol
- **Aesthetic**: Warm beige paper, bold black ink lines, monochromatic, no external watermarks.
- **Branding**: Every sketch must feature **Ghostly Shadow** as the protagonist.
- **Narrative**: Sketches are for storytelling—A to Z journeys, technical deep-dives, and roadmap visualizations.
