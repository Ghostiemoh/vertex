## Step 2: Details

**Project Details**
> Vertex is a professional-grade payments infrastructure designed to solve the fragmentation in the Solana freelance economy. freelancers currently face slow, opaque, and manual invoicing processes. Vertex brings "Surgical Finality" to these workflows by providing a platform to create wallet-signed invoices, generate cryptographic payment links (SOL, USDC, USDT), and draft formal service agreements that are executed on-chain.
>
> By leveraging the speed and low cost of the Solana Virtual Machine (SVM) alongside a robust Supabase settlement backend, Vertex ensures that every transaction occurs at the exact intersection of project agreement and financial execution. It is designed for precision, institutional-grade trust, and high-agency independent work.
