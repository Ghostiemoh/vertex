### **Project website**

### **What are you building, and who is it for?**
> Vertex is a specialized invoicing and agreement framework designed for high-agency freelancers and independent agencies. It enables users to create wallet-signed invoices, generate cryptographic payment links (SOL, USDC, USDT), and draft formal service agreements that are verified and settled on-chain with sub-second finality.

### **Why did you decide to build this, and why build it now?**
> Professional crypto workflows are currently trapped in a "fragmentation gap"—often relying on manual spreadsheets and unverified transaction hashes. With the surge in the Solana freelance economy and the maturation of the SVM, there is an urgent need for institutional-grade tools that bridge the intersection of project agreement and financial execution. We decided to build this now to give independent workers the same technical clarity and certainty as global institutions.

### **What technologies are you using or integrating with to build your product?**
> Vertex is built on a high-performance stack including:
> - **Next.js 16 (App Router)** for a snappy, modern web experience.
> - **Solana Web3.js & Wallet Adapters** for deep on-chain integration.
> - **Supabase** (Postgres + RLS) for secure settlement logs and server-side signature verification.
> - **Tailwind CSS v4 & Framer Motion** for a high-agency, professional aesthetic.
> - **Remotion** for automated, cinematic marketing and onboarding assets.
